The base website atlas uses the original approved drawings, resized to
128px cells and repacked into lossless WebP atlases. The optional `oma`
costume adds separate wig, glasses and floral coat drawings to
those same cels before their existing animation deformation. The base figure
is unchanged. Costume artwork and registration data also use CC BY 4.0.
The costume hair and fabric parts were painted with AI assistance and
registered independently; SVG glasses and collar preserve their precise fit.
The original Kurt drawings were developed with AI assistance and then
registered and refined for consistent animation.
