Meet Kurt.

A hand-drawn pug for your website. He wanders, watches and takes his time.

Copy the kurt/ folder to your website and add:

<script type="module" src="/kurt/kurt-element.mjs"></script>
<kurt-companion></kurt-companion>

This decorative embed supports pause, smaller screens and reduced motion. It stops when the tab or element is hidden. No tracking, cookies or external runtime services.
